Q: Who is Ratan Tata?
A: Ratan Naval Tata was an Indian industrialist and philanthropist. He was chairman of Tata Sons and the Tata Group from 1991 to 2012, and interim chairman from October 2016 to February 2017.

Q: When and where was Ratan Tata born?
A: Ratan Tata was born on 28 December 1937 in Bombay (now Mumbai), India.

Q: Who were Ratan Tata's parents?
A: His parents were Naval Tata and Sooni Tata. After his parents separated, he was raised mainly by his grandmother, Navajbai Tata.

Q: Where did Ratan Tata study?
A: Ratan Tata studied architecture at Cornell University in the USA, graduating in 1962. He later attended the Advanced Management Program at Harvard Business School in 1975.

Q: When did Ratan Tata join the Tata Group?
A: Ratan Tata joined the Tata Group in 1962. He started on the shop floor at Tata Steel in Jamshedpur, working alongside the workers.

Q: When did Ratan Tata become chairman of the Tata Group?
A: Ratan Tata became chairman of Tata Sons in 1991, succeeding J. R. D. Tata. He served until December 2012.

Q: Who succeeded Ratan Tata as chairman?
A: Cyrus Mistry succeeded Ratan Tata in December 2012. After Mistry was removed in 2016, Ratan Tata returned as interim chairman until N. Chandrasekaran took over in February 2017.

Q: What was Ratan Tata's role after retirement?
A: After retiring, Ratan Tata became Chairman Emeritus of Tata Sons and continued as chairman of the Tata Trusts, which own a majority stake in Tata Sons.

Q: How did the Tata Group grow under Ratan Tata?
A: Under Ratan Tata, the Tata Group grew from a mainly Indian business into a global group. Most of its revenue came to be earned outside India.

Q: What is the Tetley acquisition?
A: In 2000, Tata Tea acquired Tetley, the British tea company. It was one of the first large overseas acquisitions by an Indian company.

Q: What is the Corus acquisition?
A: In 2007, Tata Steel acquired Corus, the Anglo-Dutch steel maker, making Tata Steel one of the largest steel producers in the world.

Q: What is the Jaguar Land Rover acquisition?
A: In 2008, Tata Motors bought Jaguar and Land Rover from Ford. Jaguar Land Rover later became one of the most profitable parts of Tata Motors.

Q: What is the Tata Nano?
A: The Tata Nano was a small car launched by Tata Motors in 2009, promoted as the world's cheapest car at about one lakh rupees. Ratan Tata wanted to give families riding on two-wheelers a safe and affordable car.

Q: What is the Tata Indica?
A: The Tata Indica, launched in 1998, was the first passenger car designed and developed in India by Tata Motors.

Q: What is Tata Consultancy Services?
A: Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is the Tata Group's IT services company. It went public in 2004 under Ratan Tata's leadership and became one of India's most valuable companies.

Q: What is the Tata Trusts?
A: The Tata Trusts are the Tata Group's charitable trusts. They fund work in health, education, rural development and disaster relief, and own a majority stake in Tata Sons.

Q: What is Ratan Tata's philanthropy?
A: Ratan Tata was known for his philanthropy through the Tata Trusts. He supported cancer care, education and scholarships, including an endowment at Cornell University for Indian students.

Q: How did Ratan Tata respond to the 2008 Mumbai attacks?
A: After the November 2008 terror attacks, which included the Taj Mahal Palace hotel owned by the Tata Group, Ratan Tata ensured support for affected employees and their families.

Q: What awards did Ratan Tata receive?
A: Ratan Tata received the Padma Bhushan in 2000 and the Padma Vibhushan in 2008, two of India's highest civilian honours.

Q: What startups did Ratan Tata invest in?
A: After retirement, Ratan Tata invested personally in many Indian startups, including Ola, Paytm, Snapdeal, Urban Ladder and Lenskart.

Q: Was Ratan Tata married?
A: No. Ratan Tata never married and had no children.

Q: What is Ratan Tata known for personally?
A: Ratan Tata was known for his humility, simple lifestyle, ethics in business and his love for dogs, including the stray dogs cared for at Bombay House.

Q: When did Ratan Tata die?
A: Ratan Tata died on 9 October 2024 in Mumbai, at the age of 86. He was given a state funeral.

Q: What is Ratan Tata's leadership style?
A: Ratan Tata led with long-term vision and strong values. He emphasised ethics, trust and taking bold global bets, while keeping the Tata Group's social commitment.
